Who we work with

Is URAB Digital right for you?

We build simple static websites: fast, secure and easy to look after. Here is who we can work with, and what we leave to others.

A good fit

  • Small local businesses: décor & events, bakeries, boutiques, salons, tuition centres
  • Home and handmade businesses that sell through Instagram or WhatsApp
  • Anyone who needs an online presence: who you are, your work, reviews and how to reach you
  • Businesses that take orders and enquiries by chat, call or email

What we don’t do

  • Online payments, payment gateways or checkout
  • E-commerce stores with carts, stock or order tracking
  • Customer logins, accounts or databases
  • Mobile apps or custom software
